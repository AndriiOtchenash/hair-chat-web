## I am 18 or older

I confirm that I am at least 18 years old.

This service is for adults only. If you are under 18, please do not use it.

<!-- consent:health_data -->

## Hair and scalp data

I agree to the processing of information about my hair and scalp, which may count as health data.

In the conversation you may mention hair loss, itching, dandruff, scalp irritation, medicines or treatments. Under Article 9 GDPR such information may be health data, so we process it only with your explicit consent.

- **Why:** to give hair-care advice that takes your hair type and concerns into account.
- **What is stored:** your messages and a short hair profile derived from them.
- **What we do not do:** we do not diagnose, prescribe treatment or dosages. For worrying symptoms the assistant will recommend seeing a trichologist or a doctor.

<!-- consent:ai_processing -->

## Answers come from artificial intelligence

I understand that an AI answers me and agree that my messages are processed by external AI providers.

- Answers are generated by an AI assistant, not a human. It can be wrong; its advice is cosmetic, not medical.
- To produce an answer, your message and the conversation context are sent to the model provider: **Google (Gemini)**. **Groq** is used only in the development environment, never with user data.
- Every message is checked by the **OpenAI** moderation service to filter out harmful content.
- These providers may process data outside the EU (for example, in the USA).
- Conversations are kept for **30 days** and then deleted automatically.
- You can withdraw any consent in the settings at any time and ask us to delete your data. After withdrawal the chat becomes unavailable; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.
